# Spec-Driven Development

## Method

1. Apply this method to the current delegated outcome when authority is missing, false, materially incomplete, or subject to an important unresolved coverage question. Load through Pi's normal Skill mechanism as primary, companion, or a conditionally required method. Settled evidence permits direct entry to a narrow owning writer without waiving assessment, authority, or authorization duties; SDD is not the exclusive ADR entrance or a mandatory reload at every stage or Handler call. Apply the limited existing-authorization exceptions below only when their conditions hold.
2. Identify applicable durable authority:
   - PRD owns product intent, users, scope, outcomes, and success criteria;
   - ADR owns accepted architectural or governance decisions and rationale;
   - Spec owns normative behavior, interfaces, constraints, and failures.
3. Inspect current authority and implementation. Return material unsettled intent, alternatives, and evidence gaps for Router-owned user communication; do not ask the user or silently settle normative choices. Separate intended behavior, observed behavior, and assumptions. Before dependent planning, account visibly for every PRD/ADR/Spec family with `reuse` (adequate coverage), `update` (same owner or decision identity needs amendment), `create` (missing required authority or distinct accepted ADR lifecycle), `skip` (no change needed or not applicable), or `blocked` (unresolved coverage, ownership, evidence, permission, or intent), owning boundary, existing or intended path, and a one-sentence evidence-backed reason. Unknown ownership is unresolved; intended outputs are not readable references. Assessment supplies neither writing permission nor implementation confirmation. Reuse reliable assessment covering current scope, decisions, owners, and relevant authority versions; material changes, contradictions, or important uncertainty require reassessment only of the affected portion.
4. Within the current predicate and explicit authorization, use `documentation` and the applicable owning writers to reconcile only authority that would otherwise be false, missing, or materially incomplete. Apply `documentation-adr-writing` lifecycle: update the owning ADR for the same decision identity; create only for a distinct accepted lifecycle. Never record an unaccepted choice as an accepted ADR.
5. Required reconciliation must be complete before dependent planning. Report `blocked` for missing required coverage, ownership, evidence, permission, or intent; do not encode gaps as assumptions. Express agreed new behavior verifiably in the owning Spec before deriving an implementation Plan. Initial assessment, explicitly authorized authority writing, and first Work/Context/Plan/Waves preparation use standalone with current readable inputs and declared output targets, not the future tuple they create; never relabel graph-bound implementation standalone to bypass confirmation, prerequisites, or gates.
6. Only when derivation is the current delegated predicate, use `documentation-work`, `plan-implementation`, and their owning writers for bounded Work Context, Plan, and Waves. Use the historical Tasks compatibility Skill only for an explicit migration; do not create a new Tasks layer.
7. Implement only after the Router has disclosed and obtained explicit current-user confirmation of the ready implementation scope. If implementation reveals an upstream authority or material design gap, stop affected work and report it for Router-owned reconciliation and confirmation of the changed portion; continue unchanged confirmed work without repeat approval.
8. Validate delivered behavior against authority and repository checks.

## Exceptions

Under existing explicit authorization, a bounded repair may restore already-authoritative behavior without a new Spec or implementation-plan confirmation: identify authority and divergence, run focused verification, and report. Ambiguous authority or new behavior follows the normal authority and confirmation sequence. During an active security, data-loss, or availability incident, take only the minimum authorized containment action needed to limit harm; check its effect, report it as temporary, and reconcile authority afterward. Destructive or externally high-impact actions still require their separate action authorization; broader follow-on repair needs its own authority and plan confirmation. Small authorized behavior-neutral maintenance may proceed without a new Spec or plan confirmation after a focused unchanged-behavior check. These exceptions grant no authorization by themselves; material behavior, scope, architecture, dependency, or risk changes follow the normal sequence.

## Completion

Complete each delegated outcome against its own authority and evidence checks; derived artifacts must not conflict with applicable authority. Require implementation checks and resolve unexplained implementation drift only when implementation is the delegated outcome. This Skill composes methods only within the current predicate; it does not authorize independent assessment, authority-writing, planning, or implementation outcomes. Do not ask the user, dispatch, schedule stages, choose continuation, or advance Result/Waves/Todo state; return evidence and gaps to the Router. Evidence-backed `reuse` or `skip` preserves the exceptions without manufacturing a new ADR, Spec, Plan, or repeat approval of unchanged confirmed work.
