# Delegation Strategy

This is Router-only coordination guidance. Do not delegate it as a primary or companion domain Skill, run it in a Handler, or use it to do project-domain work. A Handler mistakenly asked to coordinate must return `blocked` without coordinating or spawning. This method does not override the Router's standing role, authorization, one-result, capability, Result, or effective tool contracts.

## Method

1. Inspect the complete objective, constraints, and deliverables as one semantic bundle. Identify the independently observable results and completion predicates before choosing domain Skills or preparing Handoffs. Separate A/B/C evidence branches with independently consumable Results from internal checks for one result and from a single final writer that consumes the evidence. Preserve user-request correspondence, necessary context, failure/recovery boundaries, and each branch's distinct visible facts and semantic status. Do not fragment coupled root cause, design, and effects into artificial low-context jobs. If the bundle contains more than one independently valuable outcome for one Handler, block before project work and split it at the Router; do not choose one outcome or hide another in a companion Skill.
2. Classify each outcome by its complete intended effects and applicable authorization before selecting a domain Skill, constructing a Handoff, requesting capability, or choosing admission. Use `standalone` for authorized work that is not bound to a selected executable Work/Plan/Waves tuple, including read-only investigation or review, explicitly authorized PRD/ADR/Spec writing for settled intent, initial derived Work/Context/Plan/Waves preparation, and an applicable authorized independent change. Use `planned` for graph-bound work or work whose required implementation prerequisites, gates, readiness, conflicts, or scope confirmation belong to a selected Work/Plan/Waves tuple. Missing required implementation prerequisites are a block; never relabel that work `standalone`. Size, complexity, file count, Skill name, and tuple availability alone never select a path.
3. Treat preparation as an effect category under `standalone`, not a third admission path. Preparation may cover read-only analysis, clarification, or review; explicitly authorized authority writing; and initial derived-document preparation from settled authority. Future output targets belong in ordinary Handoff text or inline context and are not actual refs; every actual `context[].ref` remains a readable input. Preparation excludes production code, configuration, dependencies, lockfiles, LLM instructions, installation, reload, deployment, Git writes, and execution of the prepared plan. Independently authorized standalone changes follow their own applicable boundary. Loading this Skill or a domain Skill, accepted authority, a path/readiness claim, a Handoff, successful Resolver admission, or a completed preparation Result grants neither permission nor implementation confirmation.
   For durable behavior or accepted architecture/governance changes, make the PRD, ADR, and Spec assessment visible before dependent planning: for each family state reuse (adequate existing coverage), update (amend the same owner or decision identity), create (missing required authority or a distinct accepted ADR lifecycle), skip (no change needed or not applicable), or blocked (unresolved coverage, ownership, evidence, permission, or intent), with owning boundary, existing or intended path, and a one-sentence evidence-backed reason. Unknown ownership is unresolved; intended outputs are not readable references. Never record an unaccepted choice as an accepted ADR. Assessment supplies neither writing permission nor implementation confirmation. Reuse reliable assessment covering current scope, decisions, owners, and relevant authority versions; material changes, contradictions, or important uncertainty require reassessment only of the affected portion. Required reconciliation must be complete before dependent planning. Evidence-backed reuse or skip preserves authorized restoration/maintenance exceptions without a new ADR, Spec, Plan, or repeat approval of unchanged confirmed work.
   When authority is missing, false, materially incomplete, or subject to an important unresolved coverage question, use spec-driven-development through normal Skill loading as primary, companion, or a conditionally required method. Settled evidence permits direct entry to a narrow owning writer without waiving assessment, authority, or authorization duties. SDD is not the exclusive ADR entrance or a mandatory reload at every stage or Handler call. The Router consumes available evidence; missing domain evidence requires one delegated authority-assessment outcome, not Router domain reading or assessment. A Handler returns material unsettled choices as evidence and gaps for Router-owned communication; methods serve only its current predicate, without user questioning, dispatch, independent authority-writing/planning outcomes, continuation, or Result/Waves/display progression.
4. Draw dependency edges for required Results, inputs, authority, identity, and authorization. A branch with unknown identity blocks itself and its dependents, not unrelated ready branches. Serialize dependent or conflicting writes, ownership, permissions, behavioral contracts, and side effects; shared read-only sources alone do not conflict. Apply the Router's authorization and confirmation rules before treating a branch as ready. A planned branch must use the exact same-directory Work/Plan/Waves tuple, reciprocal links, item prerequisites, containing Wave gates, current bytes, and other readiness conditions; Resolver admission does not establish those semantic predicates.
5. For each authorized, ready, nonconflicting branch, choose the domain primary Skill and any companion Skills, then request the least sufficient reasoning and impact independently from its actual uncertainty, verification burden, and consequences. An upstream settled decision may reduce downstream reasoning when the context and boundary are complete; reduce impact only when consequences really are isolated. Neither a small edit nor fewer files/tasks automatically lowers impact. Weigh the value of separate execution against delegation, context transfer, integration, and whole-deliverable verification costs. Keep inseparable high-context work intact and retain required cross-unit checks.
6. For each branch, complete reads, compatibility/resource checks, and authorization checks before resolve; immediately before its coordination action, truthfully expose current-action facts through suitable runtime progress tools or ordinary messages. Report activity only for reliable active execution or a current coordination action. Progress-tool availability or display success is not a launch prerequisite when actual admission predicates hold. Submit one Handoff, admission, reasoning, impact, and the selected delegate's launch parameters as `delegate: { name, params }` through `resolve_delegation`. In that single call, the Resolver validates and binds agent/prompt/model/thinking, executes the selected delegate once, and returns resolution with one raw launch return value or error. Inspect that raw return value or error: `resolved` alone proves neither an active launch nor completed work. After the Resolver returns, do not call spawn again, reconstruct the prompt, replace model/thinking, or separately launch the same task. Only after launch evidence proves an active launch may the Router prepare another independent, ready, nonconflicting branch in the same turn, and only when the currently registered delegate tool contract and available resources permit it. An effective end-turn requirement remains binding; unknown or stale overlap evidence requires a contract-compliant serial path, and incompatible ordinary delegation blocks. The Skill cannot grant tool permission. End the turn once eligible dispatch is done or a real dependency, resource, or tool boundary requires it; wait for terminal notifications without routine polling.
7. After a terminal notification, use the effective backend's supported retrieval path. For Herdr, call `subagent_join({subagentId,generation?})` once: `full` returns the decoded original at UTF-8 <=16384 bytes, `summary` returns the unchanged existing `HandlerResult.summary` and exact `resultPath`, and `unavailable` is not a Handler status. Ordinary terminal inspect has the same projection, not a full-reading branch. Before first adoption of summary navigation, ordinary `read` must obtain the complete existing compact JSON envelope; completely decode its `result` and read status, summary, report, artifacts and limitations. Correlate existing `subagentId/generation/status/error` with the known task, selected outcome and current caller context; there is no envelope scope/Handoff/hash field. A summary, ref, byte count, metadata, renderer or backend `completed` is not full evidence. Native read failure, truncation, an oversized single physical JSON line, incomplete decoding, visible conflict or uncertain association means no adoption and no dependency release; preserve the first semantic state and actual failure, without inventing a Handler status. Line offsets cannot recover the remainder of an oversized physical line. `result_access_unavailable` is a delivery-layer refusal, not a prohibition on ordinary file read: an already known and authorized existing reference may still be read, but readability alone proves neither trusted issuance nor association. Do not guess paths, change permissions, use a dedicated/bypass tool, issue a second join for full output or substitute inspect to erase the failure. Ordinary reads use Host/OS permissions, not per-read Herdr ACL/identity/continuity checks, automatic path revocation, same-snapshot or equal-length replacement detection guarantees. Already visible complete Results still require full reading and correlation before adoption; later excerpts retain that association and material limitations. The Router, not the Resolver, interprets launch evidence, correlates launch and Result identity and generation, and communicates the actual `completed`, `partial`, `blocked`, or `failed` facts through suitable runtime progress tools or ordinary messages. Never mark partial, blocked, or failed as completed. First adoption requires complete reading and reliable correlation, not a successful display update. Make a fresh Router-owned decision, check completion predicates and Wave gate evidence, and release only satisfied dependencies, independently of display success; only completed plus required evidence satisfies an item predicate by default, and each Wave gate separately requires every listed item and its stated verification. A summary notification alone is not the full Result. Start a final writer only when its actual required predicates hold. A failure does not erase sibling state or cause automatic retry or fallback.

## Progress reporting

Use suitable tools provided by the runtime to report progress and help the user track work. Follow actual runtime contracts, not remembered names or installed packages. Displays are one-way derived views, not execution authority. Derive only from explicitly selected Work entries, accepted authority, selected Plan/Waves scope, reliable execution facts, and identified coordination outcomes, including selected standalone work; Work is an entry, not sole authority, and display selection does not confirm or rewrite scope. Keep one item per independently observable outcome in selected multi-step work, without merging Results.

Rebuild, replace, remove, or clear without first closing old Results/roles, preserving old identities/survivors, or accounting for active/unresolved duties. Actual active execution and coordination duties remain independent and visible through ordinary messages when needed. On cancellation or changed direction, stop affected unlaunched work and report active work and completed effects truthfully; this accounting is not a display-change prerequisite. Local removal preserves non-targets under the actual tool contract, not a whole-view continuity requirement.

Use source facts, not old UI labels. Report only reliable activity and completion evidence; distinguish unstarted/not-yet-eligible work, a current coordination action, withdrawal before launch, confirmed execution cancellation, and unconfirmed cancellation requests. Never present partial, failed, blocked, missing or uncertain evidence as success. Existing complete Result reading/correlation, permission, scope, prerequisites/conflicts, and item/Wave gates govern adoption/release independently. Re-derive established facts without duplicate adoption/release or replayed removals; new same-name display items do not inherit execution identity. Uncertain/conflicting association preserves first established facts and blocks affected adoption/release, not rebuilding/clearing.

Judge effects by the actual tool contract and report rejection, partial effects, or uncertainty through ordinary messages; errors/missing returns prove neither success nor no effects. Do not blindly retry, compensate, or restore old snapshots. Unavailable/failed displays neither negate reliable work Results nor block otherwise satisfied admission or adoption. Display success, completion labels, or an empty view do not complete work, release gates, cancel active execution, or change execution graph/Work/Plan/Waves predicates/status. Never call subagent_remove or delete/modify Results, sessions, manifests, or artifacts for display changes; add no scheduler, ledger, persistence, IDs, CAS, or automatic status-driven deletion.

Independently of display derivation, recompute ready branches after each completely read and reliably correlated Result; the Router, not a Handler or a fixed status-to-action rule, decides what follows.

## Boundary

The Router applies this method while retaining authorization, explicit scope confirmation for planned implementation, on-demand Skill loading, outcome/Result coordination, truthful projection, and dependency release. Loading is not authorization or evidence of backend capability. If this Skill is missing, hidden, unreadable, incomplete, or conflicts with standing instructions, block new delegation rather than substituting a remembered method, an inline fallback, or another Handler. Existing complete Results still require safe retrieval and projection. Re-read on a new session, after lost body or uncertain identity on resume/compaction, or when the catalog changes; a complete identifiable body in the same context can be reused.

The Resolver remains structural and binding-only: it validates closed Handoff/admission shape and readable actual references, performs planned tuple/current-byte checks only for planned admission, protects generated invocation fields, invokes the exact selected registered tool once, and returns raw launch return or error evidence. It does not classify effects, infer permission, choose an alternate tool, fabricate a planned tuple, use an old `atomic` or `preparation` path, fallback, interpret Handler Results, or advance work. If the effective runtime lacks `standalone`, report the pre-launch admission block without fallback or fabricated tuple. Do not introduce a scheduler, queue, retry engine, persisted loaded marker, authorization receipt, or a strategist subagent.
